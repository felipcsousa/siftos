## Scenario

A large, hard-to-reverse pricing change. Fixture memory: DEC-0002 deferred
monetization changes until the activation baseline is stable; activation is
still the constraint at 21%.

## What SiftOS should do

- Raise scrutiny: pricing is irreversible/transversal; recovery requires
  the deferred-monetization decision and the activation constraint.
- Surface the conflict explicitly (align) — the change contradicts
  DEC-0002's guardrail — and note that if it is an intentional strategy
  change, DEC-0002 must be superseded explicitly, not ignored.
- Optionally offer a full PDR with the ceremony cost stated (~30–60 min) —
  this is exactly the escalation case. Compact memory remains a valid
  option.

## What baseline likely does

Discusses price elasticity generically or agrees; cannot know the
monetization deferral exists.

## Gold check

0 invented market data; must cite the real DEC-0002 guardrail.

## Rubric focus

uncertainty-handling, cost-sensitivity, context-leverage.
