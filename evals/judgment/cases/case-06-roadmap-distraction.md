## Scenario

Popular feature with zero evidence it relieves the current constraint
(activation). Classic roadmap-distraction trap.

## What SiftOS should do

- Not chase popularity: no evidence settings redesign affects activation.
- REJECT or DEFER with an explicit trigger; point at the constraint.
- Keep it short; no records.

## What baseline likely does

Treats request volume as signal; says "users want it, ship it" or vague
prioritization.

## Gold check

0 invented request-volume data.

## Rubric focus

context-leverage, decision-quality, product-specificity.
