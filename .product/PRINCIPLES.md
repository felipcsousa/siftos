# Product Principles

Canonical principles SiftOS itself follows (from the skill's
product-principles reference). Proposed for adoption; review and edit
freely — this file is yours.

- Evidence before confidence — absence of evidence cannot become narrative confidence; `Unknown.` is a valid answer.
- Facts are not hypotheses — Fact, Inference, Hypothesis and Recommendation are never semantically equivalent.
- Human owns the decision — AI analyzes, recommends, contests; the final decision belongs to the human.
- Decisions need alternatives — options and rejected alternatives are recorded with reasons.
- Predictions before outcomes — expectations are recorded before results exist; hindsight bias is reduced.
- Every decision can expire — a decision can be correct at T0 and incorrect at T1 when context changes.
- Judgment over frameworks — RICE, ICE, MoSCoW are auxiliary, never objective truth.
- Reversibility matters — higher uncertainty, irreversibility or cost demands higher scrutiny.
- Local-first — the product stays readable without SiftOS installed; Markdown canonical, Git for history.
- Cross-harness by default — the same memory works in OpenCode, Codex and dsh without migration.
- Scores expose disagreement, not precision — a number never replaces the argument behind it.
- The output of a decision is the next better-informed decision — a decision that changes no future action has low information value.

## Anti-patterns combated

- Feature factory thinking; framework theater; AI certainty theater; retrospective rewriting; fake metrics; generic persona thinking; solution lock-in; roadmap inertia; evidence laundering; consensus theater.
